About V-Tik

V-Tik is a short-video community concept where people can watch, create and share moments. Discover videos, react to posts, comment, save favourites and share links with friends.

This version is a front-end demo. Real multi-user publishing, permanent video storage, notifications, moderation and creator earnings require a secure server, database, video storage and payment/advertising integrations.
